# World News — 18 November 2026

make.fun bounty [news-image-20261118](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261118): sum up the world's news of Wednesday 18 November 2026 in one image. $250 over 50 places, free entry.

## The image

`entry.png` (1024×1024, PNG) is a newspaper front page rendered from
`design.html` with a headless browser, so every word is crisp text, never
AI-drawn lettering:

- **Lead:** U.S. strikes Iran's Qeshm Island; Iran hits Kuwait and Bahrain.
- **Kerch Bridge:** Ukraine's second day of strikes partly collapses the span.
- **Shenzhen:** Xi hosts Putin a day before a planned three-way summit with Trump.
- **Alaska:** Peltola wins the Senate seat; Democrats take the chamber 56–44.
- **Ebola in Congo:** over 11,000 cases, 5,400 deaths, per the UN.
- **Also:** Cyclone Xavier hits the Cook Islands, cold kills displaced people
  in Gaza tents, the U.S. loses measles-free status after 26 years.

Sources: Reuters, Associated Press, Agence France-Presse, Just Security,
United Nations.

## The program

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`. It runs twice in the grader and writes identical bytes
both times.
